Frequently asked questions

What does support include?

Help with theme setup, bugs, and questions about included features. Custom development is not covered.

How long until I get a reply?

We usually reply within 24 hours on working days.

Will I get free updates?

Yes. Updates are free as long as you keep your license active.

Can I use the theme on multiple stores?

One license covers one end product. Please buy a separate license for each store.

What details should I share when asking for help?

Your store URL, theme version, and a short description, screenshot or video of the issue. You can attach them in the form below.
